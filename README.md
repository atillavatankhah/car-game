# Ultimate Car Racing Game

A beautiful and engaging car racing game built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. Race through traffic, collect coins, and unlock amazing cars!

## Features

- 🏎️ 10 unique cars with different speed and handling characteristics
- 💰 Coin collection system
- ❤️ Three lives system
- 🎮 Smooth controls
- 🎵 Background music and sound effects
- 🏁 Score tracking
- 🔧 Car garage with purchase and selection system
- 💥 Collision effects and visual feedback

## Setup

1. Make sure you have the following files in your game directory:
   - Images:
     - car1.png to car10.png (car sprites)
     - obstacle1.png to obstacle3.png (obstacle sprites)
     - road.png (repeating road background)
     - coin.png (coin sprite)
   - Audio:
     - background.mp3 (background music)
     - coin.mp3 (coin collection sound)
     - crash.mp3 (collision sound)
     - purchase.mp3 (car purchase sound)

2. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser to start playing!

## How to Play

- Use the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys to move your car
- Avoid obstacles to keep your hearts
- Collect coins to buy new cars
- Click the Garage button to view and purchase new cars
- Click the sound button to toggle sound on/off
- Try to achieve the highest score!

## Car List

1. Classic Racer (Free) - Balanced starter car
2. Speed Demon (1000 coins) - High speed, lower handling
3. Tank Runner (2000 coins) - Lower speed, high handling
4. Lightning (3000 coins) - Very high speed, low handling
5. Balanced Pro (4000 coins) - Good speed and handling
6. Luxury Cruiser (5000 coins) - Smooth handling focus
7. Drift King (6000 coins) - High speed and handling
8. Monster Machine (7000 coins) - Excellent handling
9. Stealth Runner (8000 coins) - Superior speed
10. Ultimate Beast (10000 coins) - Perfect stats

## Tips

- Start with the Classic Racer to learn the controls
- Save coins to buy better cars
- Different cars suit different playing styles
- Watch your hearts - you only have three!
- Try to maintain a steady position to avoid obstacles
- Use the full width of the road to collect coins

## Technical Requirements

- Modern web browser with JavaScript enabled
- Support for HTML5 Audio
- Keyboard with arrow keys
